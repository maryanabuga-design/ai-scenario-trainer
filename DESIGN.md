# DESIGN.md — AI Scenario Trainer (MVP)

Status: draft v1. Values marked (proposal) are open for change. Update this file first, then the code.

## Product and audience
- Product: AI-powered scenario-based training. The Trainee uploads their own material and practises decisions in scenarios, then gets feedback and error analysis.
- Context: educational only. Emergency-response and civil-protection training. No weapons, harm or combat content.
- Platform: mobile-first web app, designed for a 390 by 844 phone screen and usable from 360px wide. On wide screens the app is shown as a centred column about 430px wide on a neutral background (`--surface-muted`). There is no separate desktop layout.
- Scope and screens: see `docs/mvp-matrix-and-ia.md` and the screen map below.

## Principles
1. Calm, warm and focused. Trust matters more than delight.
2. Hierarchy comes from type, spacing and contrast, not from color.
3. Black is the action colour. One accent (indigo) for focus and links. Semantic colours only for status, always paired with an icon and a text label.
4. No gradients and no decorative shapes. No glows, mascots, emoji, 3D icons or decorative illustration.
5. AI shows itself through behavior (a source citation, stated uncertainty, an honest "not covered"), not through sparkles or orbs.
6. Readable under stress: large targets, short copy, one primary action per screen.

Rules:
- Accent must not be green or red (those carry meaning).
- The saturated status green and red are reserved for the answer result: `--correct` / `--correct-bg` mean "Correct" and `--wrong` / `--wrong-bg` mean "Incorrect". Error types use their own muted tints (sage, warm grey, soft amber, soft rose), always with their own icon and name, so a type is never read from its colour. Material status (14 Oct 2026) also uses the status green for Ready and the error red for Failed, always with its own icon and label (see StatusChip), so it never reads as an answer result. The status green stays clearly apart from the olive of the gradient.
- Status = icon + label + color. Never color alone.
- Tags are categories, never status. A material's type is not a tag: it is the icon and plain label on its material tile (see "Material types"). Every tag ("SCENARIO", "QUIZ" and the rest) uses the one neutral surface. The status green (`--correct` #1F7A4D on `--correct-bg`, always with a check icon and the word "Correct") is far more saturated than the grey-green sage, so a file tag never reads as a result. Status green, bright green and pink are not tag colours, so tags cannot be read as correct or wrong; the sage tag is grey-green and muted.
- Dark theme is out of scope for the MVP.

## Spacing, shape, depth
- Spacing scale (4px base): 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48.
- Radius: 24px cards and sheet top corners, 16px inner panels, options and inputs, full pill for buttons, tags and the current nav item. Borders 1px `--border` where a card sits on `--surface`.

- No shadows except a subtle one on overlays (sheet, dialogs).

## Colour system (locked 13 Oct 2026)
Primary = deep green. Secondary = sand.
- Primary `--primary` #3F4638 (deep olive-green): filled primary buttons and selected controls. Pressed: `--primary-pressed` (#34392F, the same green mixed 30% towards `--text`); white on it 11.85:1.
- Secondary `--secondary-sand` #F0ECE6 (a warm sandy beige, measured from the earlier Your choice fill; replaces the sage `--secondary-olive` #DFE5D8 of the previous step): inactive states and tonal backgrounds. Pressed, strokes and inactive dots: `--secondary-sand-pressed` #E2DED5 (the same warm hue about 6 points darker in lightness; the existing tone of `--border`).
- Surroundings measured from the tokens: page `--bg` #F3EEE9, cards `--card` #FAF7F3, cards and dialogs `--surface` #FFFFFF.
- Sand against them: 1.02:1 on the page, 1.10:1 on cards, 1.18:1 on the white sheet, so a sand shape is nearly invisible on its own. Wherever sand sits on the page or a card (the segmented control track, Guide chips, the Your choice block, disabled buttons), it gets a 1px `--secondary-sand-pressed` stroke; inactive progress segments, dots and the empty part of the progress ring are filled with `--secondary-sand-pressed` (1.16:1 on the page, 1.26:1 on cards, 1.34:1 on white). Meaning is always carried by a label or the active state, never by the colour alone.
- Contrast on `--secondary-sand`: `--text` 14.79:1, `--text-secondary` (muted labels) 5.85:1, `--primary` 8.32:1, the focus ring 8.18:1, the red cross 5.55:1, the green check 4.52:1. On `--secondary-sand-pressed`: `--text` 12.96:1, `--text-secondary` 5.13:1.
- Used for: Guide chips; the Your choice block (with its 3px dark green line); inactive step segments, carousel and onboarding dots, thin progress tracks and the empty part of the progress ring (pressed tone); the segmented control track; disabled button and option backgrounds with a muted label; the pressed state of outline buttons, option chips and text buttons.
- Not used for (unchanged on purpose): the coloured tags (SCENARIO yellow, QUIZ blue, SHORT peach, FULL lilac, EXTENDED blue, TRAINING green, Progress mode tags, the direction chip, all without a stroke), the icon backgrounds (topic tiles, What to create tiles, the Next up tile), error-type chips and rows, selected cards (the sage tint `--next-card` with the `--stroke-sage` stroke, pressed `--secondary-pressed`: their own tokens), the white circular back button and the white arrow circle on the Next up card, primary buttons, neutral status chips (Ready, Processing, Marked correct by you) and neutral panels such as empty-state circles and chat bubbles, which use `--surface-muted`.

## Pastel palette (15 Oct 2026; replaces "Chip tones")
ONE palette of soft pastels for every multi-colour background: chips and tags, the icon tiles (topic tiles on Home and Weak topics, the "Next up", Create training and onboarding tiles, the error-type tiles on the results), the Progress chart fills and the current-item pill in the bottom navigation. No screen sets its own pastel; nothing else in the app has an older pastel or saturated value (the former `--chip-*` fills, `--tag-*`, `--pastel-green`, `--pastel-yellow`, `--secure-bg`, `--caution-bg`, `--misconception-bg` and `--guess-tile` are removed). The file-type tiles on Materials and the S1 to S10 circles keep their own `--olive-tint` #D6DEC9. A chip inside a SelectableRow does not change when the row is selected.

Chips have no stroke: a soft pastel fill, dark text or icon, nothing else. Every chip is drawn by the one shared Chip component (the Tag, the status chip and the Guide chip only choose a tone and a size). Chips keep the meaning of their hue: Ready green with a check, Failed rose with an alert icon, Processing sand with a clock, each error type in its own tone. Interactive chips (the Guide chip) have only the keyboard focus ring and no hover.

- The six requested values (yellow #F8F2CC, rose #F9E5E3, blue #DFECF0, lilac #EDE7F5, green #DFEDE5, peach #F7E1D8) were checked in CIE76 ΔE against the three backgrounds above and against the app's own #F3EEE9, #FAF7F3 and #F2F3EE. Yellow, blue, lilac and peach passed as given. Rose (5.99 on #F3EEE9) and green (5.78 on #F2F3EE) fell just below 6, so each was darkened by the smallest step that passes: rose to #F8E4E2 (1 per channel, lowest ΔE 6.1) and green to #DDEBE3 (2 per channel, lowest ΔE 6.2). The sand stays #E5DFD4 (it was darkened from #ECE6DB earlier because #ECE6DB was only 4.3 from the page).
- Rule: every tone is at least 6 in CIE76 ΔE from each of those six backgrounds, so a chip or tile never gets lost on the page or a card. The tones sit within 7 points of the same lightness (L* 89 to 96) so none looks stronger than the rest.
- Text and icon contrast on every tone is at least 4.5:1 (the lowest is Ready's green on green at 4.80:1). On icon tiles the icon is always the dark `--text` colour.
- Where each tone is used (hue meaning stays as before): topic tiles cycle yellow, blue, lilac, green, peach by guide section; the Next up, Create training and onboarding tiles are green; the error-type tiles on the results are green (Secure), sand (Guess), yellow (Knowledge gap) and rose (Misconception).

## Gradients
- None. The app has no gradients (the evacuation guide's gradient cover was removed on 13 Oct 2026; every material uses the material tile (olive tint)). Home has no graphic elements at the top, and the Results header uses the soft light header (see "Results screen").

## Material tile
- One tile for every material, on the Materials list (Ready, Processing and Failed), the material page header and the upload sheet's sample files: a 64px rounded square, 20px radius, filled with `--olive-tint` (#D6DEC9), the same for PDF, DOCX, LINK and every other file type. The type is told by the icon and the plain label (see "Material tile content"), never by the colour. Static, decorative (`aria-hidden`), no gradient, no shapes.
- Contrast: the dark green icon and label (`--primary`) on the tile 7.07:1 (AA for the 12px label), `--text` 12.56:1. The tile against the near-white card (#FAF7F3) is a 1.30:1 step in lightness; it is a decorative fill, so the 3:1 rule for meaningful graphics does not apply, and the icon on it carries the information.

## Layout
- Header with an action (one shared component, HeaderRow): a row with the title on the left and the button on the right, vertically centred, 12px apart; the button never wraps its label and never shrinks, the title takes the rest and may wrap. The subtitle is always a separate line below the whole row, 12px under it, never beside or under the button, and may wrap. 24px from the header to what follows (tabs, list). Used by the Materials page, the upload sheet, the Ask AI sheet, the source sheet and the soft cards with an action (Error types → Details).
- Single column with 16px side padding. Content width is the screen width, 430px maximum.
- Safe areas: respect the top and bottom safe areas (`env(safe-area-inset-top)` and `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`) on every fixed element.
- Bottom navigation: four sections, Home, Materials, Progress and Profile. It is shown only on these four sections.
- Onboarding, the scenario session and Session results are opened from other screens. They have no navigation item and no bottom navigation. Screens below another screen show the circular back button at the top left (see Circular back button); a running session shows "Exit" in its top bar instead. Exit asks for confirmation (Stay or Leave); the session stays saved and Home offers to continue it.
- Primary action: a fixed bar at the bottom of the screen with one olive pill button. On the four sections it sits above the bottom navigation; elsewhere it sits at the bottom. Scrolling content gets bottom padding so nothing hides behind the bar.
- During a session: see "Session screens (v2)": Exit, the position, the step strip and the bottom bar with Back and Next or Finish.
- Sources: the source panel is a bottom sheet opened from a source chip.

## Content and tone
- Calm, direct, sentence case. No emoji, no exclamation marks, no jokes.
- Short labels. Say what happened and what to do next.
- AI states uncertainty plainly and always points to the source passage, or says the material does not cover it.

## Accessibility
- WCAG AA: 4.5:1 text, 3:1 for large text and UI boundaries.
- Focus: one app focus ring, 2px `--accent` (#3D3A8C), 2px offset, shown only for keyboard focus (`:focus-visible`), never after a mouse click or a tap. The app remembers how the person last interacted: a pointer press (mouse, finger, pen) switches rings off, and a navigation key (Tab, the arrow keys, Home, End, Page Up, Page Down, Escape) switches them back on, so a ring never appears on a chip, button, card or link after a tap, even when focus is moved by the app (for example back to a Guide chip when the source sheet closes). This replaces every browser default outline. Keyboard focus stays clearly visible: the ring is 8.35:1 against the page, 9.01:1 on cards, 9.62:1 on white, 6.09:1 on the Guide chip and 8.63:1 on the Next up card.
- Everything operable by keyboard. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Minimum touch target 44×44px.

## Do / Don't
- Do: use whitespace and type size to create hierarchy.
- Do: show the source next to every AI claim.
- Do: keep the screens flat; there is no decoration.
- Don't: use identical cards without hierarchy, random gradients or decorative icons.
- Don't: celebrate with confetti or mascots; use a neutral confirmation instead.

## Question model and session (v3, 16 Oct 2026; replaces Session screens v2, error types, confidence, explanations and the answer sheet)
Serious, institutional tone. Scenario and quiz are ONE pattern: a question with 3 or 4 options (two for true or false), one correct answer. The person either knows it or not, so the result of a question is only correct or incorrect. During a session nothing says which; all feedback is in Results and the Answer review.

Removed completely: the answer bottom sheet, the confidence question, the free-text explanation and its keyword check and verdicts, "Better explanation", "My explanation (or answer) is still correct" with Undo, the error types (Secure, Guess, Misconception, Knowledge gap), their chips, the Error types card, the Error types details and Error analysis pages, multiple choice, ordering and short text questions, and the content fields `explanationKeyBeats`, `explanationKeywords` and `referenceExplanation`. Any question that was not single choice is single choice now (ids kept). Where wording mentioned error types it is plain: "6 topics to repeat".

Shared data shape (scenario steps and quiz questions): `{ id, section, situation?, question, options[], correctOptionId, why, guideRef, image?, hint?, type? }`. `section` is the guide section the item counts for (a step's first source); `guideRef` is the list of guide sections shown as Guide chips; `situation` exists on scenario steps only; `image` is `{ src, alt }`; `hint` is an optional string; `type` is "single" (the default) or "true-false". Option counts: a scenario question has exactly 3 options (A, B, C); a quiz question has 3 or 4 options, or 2 options for true-false ("True" and "False", letters A and B). A scenario step also keeps a short `title` for lists. One shared screen component draws both.

Hints. A hint reminds the person what to think about and points to the guide section; it never states, quotes or reveals the correct answer (a content test fails if a hint contains the exact text of the correct option). Without a `hint`, the fallback is "Review: <section title>" built from `guideRef`. With neither, the hint button is hidden for that question. Opening a hint is recorded on the answer as `hintUsed: true`, for the admin progress later.

Flow. Choose an option, then Next (Finish on the last), disabled until an option is chosen. Answers are saved as they are given. At Finish the result is calculated from the final answers (the last choice for each question) and Results open.

Weak topics. A weak topic is a guide section where at least one answer was wrong, counted from the latest answer to each question. A repeat round brings back the questions answered wrong in the chosen sections.

Storage. Saved data is versioned (`schema` 6; 5 added the exam history fields and the exam key `ast.exam.v1`; 6 adds a content fingerprint). Versions 3 and 4 only add fields (`hintUsed`, the saved question ids, and the stored part results, the last chosen Training mode and `partId` and `returnTo` on a session), so a value saved with `schema` 2 to 5 is read as it is and written back as 6; a value of any other version, or one that does not parse, is ignored: it reads as nothing and never crashes. Unfinished sessions (answers and the current step) are saved for the Home "continue" block. Saved (bookmarked) question ids are kept under their own key and are not deleted by Reset progress. Content fingerprint (17 Oct 2026): every saved value also carries a fingerprint of the question ids in the content. When it differs from the current one (the content was replaced or extended, or the value is older than schema 6), the value is cleaned as it is read: ids of steps and questions that no longer exist are dropped from answers, histories, part results, saved questions and the exam, a session or exam left with no valid question disappears (so Home and Training never show a broken Continue), and a part result that refers only to unknown ids is ignored. Valid answers stay. A lookup used for saved data returns nothing for an unknown id and never throws. Data that cannot be read or cleaned (corrupted JSON, the wrong shape) reads as an empty state instead of crashing the app. See `docs/content-and-saved-data.md`.

## Training screen (17 Oct 2026; replaces Session setup)
PRINCIPLE: the main learning flows are not configurable. The admin provides materials and the learner studies them as they are; the only configurable session is the mini-quiz (its own screen). Session setup is gone: no length 10, 20 or 30, no Shuffle, no "Only topics to repeat", no Guide sections screen, no setup hero. Session length is fixed: a part session has the questions of that part, a repeat round the wrong questions, a mini-quiz 5, 10 or 15.

Content model (17 Oct 2026). Each topic (S1 to S10) has ONE scenario (one developing story) and ONE quiz. The target is 30 questions per topic and mode and never fewer than 20; where the guide cannot support 20 distinct questions without padding, the maximum it supports is written and the shortfall is listed in `docs/content-gaps.md`. A scenario step has a `situation` (2 to 4 sentences building on the previous step), a `question`, exactly 3 options, one `correctOptionId`, a `why`, `guideRef`, a `hint` (never the answer), a `scene` id and an `image` (empty `src`, a descriptive `alt`); a new scene starts about every 5 steps and consecutive steps of a scene share the same image. A quiz question has 3 or 4 options, or is true or false (two options, "True" and "False", about one in five), a `why` and `guideRef`, in a fixed order. Every fact comes from the guide text.
Parts. Topics are the guide sections S1 to S10. For each mode (scenario and quiz) and each section that has questions in that mode, the section's questions are split into chunks of `PART_SIZE` = 10 (one named constant; changed from 5 on 17 Oct 2026): scenario chunks follow story order, quiz chunks follow the fixed bank order. A section with fewer than 10 questions has one part, 20 questions give two parts and 30 give three. Parts are derived, not stored as content. Part id: `<mode>:<sectionId>:<n>`, n from 1. Sections without questions in a mode are not listed in that mode.

Stored per part (own key, inside the versioned schema, written only when a part session FINISHES; an unfinished session counts toward nothing but "Continue"): `lastPercent`, `attempts`, `answeredIds` and `correctIds` of the latest finished attempt.

Selectors (the readiness selector is kept for Progress later; Home no longer shows it): answered = unique questions of the mode that have a latest finished answer; total = the questions of the mode in S1 to S10 (10 scenario steps, 33 quiz questions); accuracy = correct unique divided by answered unique, 0% when none; part state: "not started" (no finished attempt and no unfinished session), "in progress" (an unfinished session for the part), "done" (a finished attempt); weak count per section = the questions of that section whose latest answer is wrong. A finished session of any kind (part, repeat round, a mini-quiz later) updates the stats; only a finished part session updates a part ring.

## Layout rules (17 Oct 2026)
- Every screen is inside the page container: `AppShell` gives `main` the page side gutter, `--gutter` (16px), left and right, at 360px and at every wider width (the app is a centred column of at most `max-w-app`). Titles, subtitles, rows, cards and the sticky bottom buttons all stay inside it.
- Components must not override it: no negative outer margins on a row or card, no own outer width, no `100vw`. The only things allowed to reach the screen edges are marked `data-full-bleed`: the horizontal scrollers (the step strip, the Ask AI chips, which start at the gutter and scroll under the edges) and the background of the sticky bottom bar, whose content is still inside the gutter. The small `-ml-3` / `-mr-3` on tertiary text buttons only cancel their invisible 12px tap padding, so the visible text sits exactly on the gutter.
- The gutter is always written with its fallback, `var(--gutter, 1rem)` (as `px-[var(--gutter,1rem)]`), never as a bare `var(--gutter)`: a style sheet that is stale or missing the token (a dev server that has not picked up a change of `globals.css`) then still gives 16px instead of 0.
- A SelectableRow has the list-card radius (`rounded-card`, `--radius-card` 24px, the same as every card) and, inside the page gutter, padding equal to the gutter on all four sides. A row with no padding would look like a pill; the browser tests catch both.
- Guarded by `e2e/layout.spec.ts`: at 360px and 430px, on every main screen, no visible text, card, row or button comes closer than the gutter to either screen edge, and the page never scrolls sideways.

## Exam (17 Oct 2026)
A full check of the knowledge, opened from the Exam tile on Home. It lives under `/exam` and never creates an unfinished session for Home or Training: the running exam has its own storage key (`ast.exam.v1`).
- Constants (never hard-coded in the UI): `EXAM_QUESTIONS = 20`, `EXAM_MINUTES = 20`, `EXAM_WARNING_MINUTES = 2` and the pass mark `PASS_MARK` (80).
- Intro (`/exam`, no bottom navigation): the white circular Back (to Home), the title "Exam" (page title) and the muted line "A full check of your knowledge". A card "How it works" with four rows, each with a 36px olive-tint circle and a 18px icon: "20 questions from all topics", "20 minutes", "No hints", "Pass mark 80%". A card "Your results": the last result and the best result (percent and date, for example "Last: 70% · 14 Oct" and "Best: 85% · 12 Oct"), or "You have not taken the exam yet". A sticky Primary "Start exam".
- Question selection: EXAM_QUESTIONS questions of the selected guide (S1 to S10), a random mix of scenario and quiz questions (half and half when both exist), at least one question of every topic when possible, the rest random without repeats; with fewer questions all of them are used; random order.
- Session (`/exam/session`): the same question screens, with these differences: no hint button (the bottom bar shows only the Primary Next / Finish); under the top row a right-aligned countdown pill "mm:ss" (a `--card` pill with a clock icon; in the last 2 minutes it is a rose pastel tone with the dark text) in a polite live region that only announces the last two minutes; at 0:00 the exam finishes by itself with the answers so far, and unanswered questions count as incorrect. The bookmark, the step strip and Back to change answers work as in other sessions. The end time is stored, so a refresh keeps the timer (an exam whose time ran out while the page was closed finishes when the page opens).
- Leaving: Exit, and Back on the first question, open "Leave the exam?" with "Your progress will be lost.", a Primary "Stay" and a Tertiary "Leave". Leaving saves no result and the exam cannot be continued.
- Results (`/exam/results`): as before (Passed or Not passed yet, percent, pass mark marker, Weak topics card, Review answers, Repeat weak topics) with the overline "Exam"; the Back circle returns to the Exam intro. Review answers has its own pages (`/exam/results/review/N`).
- History: each finished exam is added to the history with its date, percent, number of questions and time used. The intro shows the last and the best result and the Home tile the best. A finished exam counts toward the answered questions, the accuracy and the readiness (the same unique-question rules), but not toward any part ring and not toward the Today goal. Only answered questions are added to the answers of the history.

## Mini-quiz screen (17 Oct 2026)
The ONLY place in the app where the learner configures a session. Quiz questions only, never scenarios, from the guide's sections S1 to S10. Entry points: the Mini-quiz card on the Training screen (Quiz mode) and the Mini-quiz row on Home; both open `/mini-quiz?from=training` or `?from=home`, and the white Back returns to that screen.
Layout (360px, page `--bg`), top to bottom, 16px side padding, 16px between blocks:
1. Top row: the white circular Back (to where it was opened from) and, beside it, the title "Mini-quiz" (item title) with the muted line "Pick topics and how many questions" (14 `--text-secondary`).
2. Card "Topics" (`--card`, 24px radius, card shadow, 16px padding): a header with the title "Topics" and a tertiary "Deselect all" / "Select all" link on the right (the same toggle as Create training: "Deselect all" when every topic is selected, "Select all" otherwise), then one SelectableRow row per section (row size, check box on the right): a 36px `--olive-tint` circle with the section number ("S3"), the section title and "N questions" in 14 `--text-secondary`. All topics are selected by default; tapping a row toggles it.
3. Card "Questions" (same card style): the title "Questions" and the amounts as a SegmentedControl in ONE row (radio): 5, 10, 15. The pool is the number of quiz questions in the selected topics. Only the amounts not larger than the pool are shown; with a pool smaller than 5 there is a single option "All N" (N = the pool); a pool larger than 5 that is not exactly an amount gets no extra option (a pool of 12 offers 5 and 10). The default is the largest available amount. When the topics change the options are recomputed; the current choice is kept if it is still offered, otherwise the largest available amount is chosen.
4. A muted line under the cards: "N questions available in your selected topics" (live region, polite).
5. Sticky bottom primary "Start mini-quiz", disabled when no topic is selected, with the muted hint "Select at least one topic" above it.
Starting. The questions are drawn at random from the pool, without repeats, and their order is random on every start (there is no shuffle control). The session is a normal quiz session on the existing question screens; it has no part, so it counts toward the Training stats (answered, accuracy) and the readiness but not toward any part ring. Results work as usual, and Back on Results returns to the screen the mini-quiz was started from (Home or Training). Starting replaces an unfinished session.
